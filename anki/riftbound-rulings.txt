Does damage lower a unit's Might?	No. Damage never reduces Might — it is marked on the unit (use counters).	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
Does a temporary '-X Might this turn' debuff defeat an already-damaged unit?	Yes. Akali 3M took 4 damage, was saved by En Garde (+2M -> 5M) with 4 damage marked, then hit by Eclipse (-4 Might this turn) -> 1M.	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
Does healing happen after a showdown even if combat damage was never calculated?	Yes. Annie put 5 damage on a 6-Might Master Yi; the attacker moved in two units and the defender wiped both with Cannon Barrage before damage was calculated.	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
Does Red Akali's in-and-out 'when I move' ping persist if she leaves the showdown?	No. If she is the only unit, leaving ends the showdown and the damage heals — you cannot stack chip damage by bouncing alone.	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
Does focus pass automatically when a chain resolves?	Yes — and this is widely misplayed. Focus passes to the other player any time a chain resolves; you cannot resolve your own spell and immediately fire another.	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
Can you play an action after passing once in a showdown?	Yes. Attacker may act; if they decline or their action resolves, defender may; then back to attacker, repeating until BOTH pass consecutively, at which point combat damage is dealt.	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
Are 'Add'-type energy abilities reactable?	No. Exhausting something like Lux, Crownguard for energy behaves like tapping runes — it is not reactable and puts nothing on the chain.	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
If a Might debuff expires and a unit crosses back above the threshold, does it 'become Mighty' again?	Yes, and it re-triggers 'when I become Mighty' effects.	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
Does Stun prevent a unit from gaining Might?	No. Stun reads only 'it doesn't deal combat damage this turn' and has zero interaction with gaining or losing Might.	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::reddit-field-notes riftbound::topic::reddit
Does Abandoned Hall's ability trigger if the spell is countered?	No. Abandoned Hall's "when a player plays a spell" condition is fulfilled only when that spell finishes resolving.<br><br><small>Core Rules 419.4.a, 419.4.a.1</small>	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::cards riftbound::topic::abandoned-hall
Can a unit moved into or away from Abandoned Hall by a spell be targeted?	After the spell resolves, Abandoned Hall can choose only a unit at Abandoned Hall controlled by the player who played that spell.<br><br><small>Core Rules 190.6.c, 355.10, 355.9.b, 383.3, 401.1, 402.2, 419.4.a</small>	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::cards riftbound::topic::abandoned-hall
Can Abandoned Hall's triggered ability choose a unit that a spell plays there?	Yes, if the player who played the spell controls that unit when the ability finalizes.<br><br><small>Core Rules 190.6.c, 337.1.b, 337.2, 354.2, 354.3, 355.10, 355.9.b, 359.2.c, 383.3, 401.1, 402.2, 419.4.a</small>	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::cards riftbound::topic::abandoned-hall
If my Dazzling Aurora plays Akshan, Mischievous at the end of my turn, will the Dazzling Aurora I take from my opponent trigger that turn?	No. You gain control of the enemy Dazzling Aurora after its end-of-turn trigger condition has already been checked.<br><br><small>Core Rules 191.4.a, 317.1.a, 383.1, 383.2.c, 383.4.a, 383.4.a.2, 406.5</small>	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::cards riftbound::topic::akshan-mischievous
If my opponent and I each use Akshan, Mischievous to take the same gear, who controls it when either Akshan dies?	The most recently played Akshan takes precedence while both are on the board.<br><br><small>Core Rules 390.4, 391, 428.2, 477.1.a, 478, 480, 480.1, 480.3</small>	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::cards riftbound::topic::akshan-mischievous
How are targets chosen when playing Alpha Strike?	Alpha Strike's controller chooses its targets while finalizing the spell, in the following order.<br><br><small>Core Rules 355.10, 355.14.a, 355.14.b, 355.14.c, 355.15, 355.5, 355.8, 355.9.b, 356.2.a.2, 740.1.b, 809.1.c</small>	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::cards riftbound::topic::alpha-strike
Does Applied Researchers reduce Repeat, Flow, and Deflect costs?	Yes, provided Applied Researchers is Empowered and the cost belongs to a spell.<br><br><small>Core Rules 356.1, 356.4, 356.4.f, 809.1.d, 820.1, 820.1.a, 828.1.b.1, 829.1.c.1</small>	riftbound::rules riftbound::rules::cards riftbound::topic::applied-researchers
